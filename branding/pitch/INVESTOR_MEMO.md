## The one-liner

AI Founder Hub is the community where non-technical founders, freelancers, and
operators learn to build and ship real AI products. We monetize through a revenue
ladder (free workshops, a $49.99/mo membership, a $249 bootcamp, and $299/hr 1:1
sessions), and we run our own growth on AI agents so customer acquisition stays
structurally cheap.

**The ask: 100,000 AED for 20% equity** (about $27,200 USD, a 500,000 AED / ~$136K
post-money valuation). Open to a larger check for a larger stake to scale faster.

## The opportunity

The bottleneck to building software has moved. It is no longer writing code, it is
knowing what to build and how to direct AI to build it. That shift creates a large,
fast-growing market of people who suddenly *can* build but do not yet know *how*.

- **1 billion plus workers** will need reskilling by 2030, and AI is the fastest
  growing skill category. (World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025)
- AI is projected to create a **net +78 million jobs** by 2030 (170M created, 92M
  displaced). (WEF 2025)
- **Cohort-based, community learning lifts course completion from roughly 10% to
  roughly 85%** versus self-paced video. Our format is built around this. (industry
  data via SNS Insider, 2026)
- **Agentic AI** is a brand new category: about **$7.6B in 2025, projected to reach
  $52.6B by 2030** (~47% CAGR). Running a company's growth on agents is only
  possible right now. (Grand View Research / MarketsandMarkets)
- The **creator economy** is approaching **$480B by 2027** (Goldman Sachs), converging
  with online education into paid communities.

## The differentiator: an AI growth engine

We do not just teach AI. We operate the company on it. A stack of agents runs our
marketing:

- **Content Agent**: creates and schedules social content across channels.
- **Engagement Agent**: handles community replies and DMs, converting attention to
  signups around the clock.
- **Budget Agent**: allocates paid spend, tests creative, and targets audiences to
  cut waste.

Three consequences: customer acquisition cost stays structurally low, content scales
without hiring, and the agent stack becomes a **sellable product** later through our
Automation Agency pathway. We run it on ourselves first as proof, then sell it.

## Market sizing

All SAM and SOM figures are forward estimates for a pre-launch brand with zero current
members or revenue. They represent the opportunity, not results.

- **TAM (~$50B):** the global e-learning subscription segment expected to reach about
  $50B by 2026 (Custom Market Insights), inside a $325B+ total e-learning market
  (Arizton 2025).
- **SAM (~$300M/yr, estimate):** UAE plus English-speaking GCC founders, freelancers,
  and operators. We size SAM as the annual revenue opportunity if a reachable base of
  ~500,000 people were served at our blended ~$600/year per paying member. It is a
  ceiling, not a claim that all 500,000 pay.
- **SOM (~$3M ARR, estimate):** ~1% of SAM (~5,000 paying members) over three years.
  A conservative nearer-term milestone is 1,000 members at $49.99/mo, about $600K ARR,
  plus bootcamp and 1:1 revenue.

## Financial model (conservative case)

### Assumptions (deliberately cautious)

- Pre-launch today. Free signups ramp linearly from ~80/mo (Month 1) to ~660/mo
  (Month 18). No viral spike assumed.
- **3.5%** of new free signups convert to membership (below our own 4% target).
- **8%** monthly membership churn (our KPI ceiling used as the expected case, not the
  best case). Implied member lifetime ~12.5 months.
- **2%** of signups buy a bootcamp; **1.5%** of active members book a 1:1 hour monthly.
- Lean cost base: AI tooling and APIs ~$350/mo, agent-managed paid budget ramping
  $800 to $2,500/mo, ops/contractor $400 to $700/mo, processing 4% of revenue. Minimal
  founder salary during the funded runway.
- Excluded as upside (not modeled): paid Dubai events, 1:1 bundles, corporate B2B
  deals, and selling the agent stack as a product.

### Unit economics

| Metric | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Membership ARPU | $49.99 / mo | Blended ~$82/mo at Month 12 with bootcamp + 1:1 |
| CAC (paid channel) | ~$259 | The conservative figure we underwrite to |
| CAC (blended) | ~$104 | Agent-driven organic rides free on the spend |
| Member LTV | ~$600 | 12.5-mo life at 8% churn, before upsells |
| LTV : CAC | 2.3 : 1 (paid) | Trending toward ~5.8 : 1 blended |

### 18-month projection

| Quarter | Members (exit) | MRR (exit) | Total rev | Net |

| Q1 (M1-3) | ~11 | $540/mo | $2,735 | -$2,024 (funded investment phase) |

**Run-rate by Month 18: ~$87K ARR** on ~144 active members, with ~$91K cumulative
revenue and ~$38.8K cumulative net after the early funded phase.

**Break-even:** operating break-even around **Month 5**, durably positive from Month 7.
Cumulative positive around Month 9. The early losses (~$2K through Q1) are the intended
investment phase, fully covered by the raise.

### Why 500,000 AED post-money

This is a standard pre-seed angel entry for a pre-launch but heavily de-risked asset
base. It is priced on built work, a complete and priced revenue ladder, a two-sided
founding team (business plus AI engineering), a structural differentiator in the growth
agents, and a large fast-moving market. We are not claiming a multiple on revenue that
does not exist. We are pricing the work already done, a defensible plan, and a team.

### A larger check

An additional ~150,000 AED (a 250,000 AED round) would mainly compress time-to-scale:
roughly triple the agent-managed paid budget and pull the signup ramp forward an
estimated 4 to 6 months; fund the agent stack into a sellable multi-tenant product
sooner; add the paid Dubai in-person track; and extend runway toward 24+ months,
removing the need for a bridge. We would only deploy it at a maintained or improving
LTV:CAC, not to buy growth at a loss.

## Honest risks and disclosures

- **Pre-launch.** No members, revenue, or testimonials exist yet. Every forward number
  here is a labeled estimate.
- **Execution risk on the agents.** The CAC advantage depends on the growth agents
  performing. We mitigate by leading with the conservative paid-channel LTV:CAC of
  2.3:1, which stands even if the agents only modestly outperform.
- **Single-market start.** We begin in the UAE and English-speaking GCC. Concentrated,
  but it is the best market on earth for this right now.
- **Founder-dependent.** Two founders, both full-time on AI Founder Hub from launch, lean
  by design. The raise funds tools and agents rather than headcount, which is the point,
  but it concentrates key-person risk early.
