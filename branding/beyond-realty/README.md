# Beyond Realty — Logo Kit

**Property, Redefined Globally.**

## The idea
A slender **pointed gateway arch** — a nod to Dubai's architectural language and to crossing a threshold *beyond* the ordinary. A fine **horizon line** runs through its base (global reach / land), and a small **apex point** sits above the arch like a guiding star (direction, the destination beyond). Drawn in two strokes only — restrained, confident, luxury through negative space.

## Typography
- **Wordmark "BEYOND":** Cormorant Garamond, Medium, wide tracking (high-contrast luxury serif).
- **"REALTY" + tagline:** Jost, Light, generous letter-spacing (clean modern sans).
- Both are free on Google Fonts. Substitutes: Didot / Bodoni for the serif, Futura / Montserrat Light for the sans.

## Clear space & minimum size
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the apex point on all sides.
- Don't render the full stacked lockup below ~28 mm / 110 px wide — use the mark or badge instead.
- Never recolour the mark outside the palette, stretch it, or add shadows/gradients.
